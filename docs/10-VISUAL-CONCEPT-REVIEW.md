# 10 — Visual direction review: three screen concepts

**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Status:** First visual exploration CREATED, NOT APPROVED, not coded.
**Media:** Three generated portrait mockups shared with the project owner in this ChatGPT conversation. Their generated characters are concept art only, not technically reviewed martial-arts demonstrations. The binary assets are not currently committed to GitHub.

## Visual concepts
1. Training Home — cinematic full-body trainer art, strong Boxing Foundations hierarchy, immediate next lesson, visual learning path and Continue Training CTA.
2. Skill Studio — large trainer area, anatomical callouts, angle-selection concept, step-by-step explanation and access to a knowledge check.
3. Guided Practice — active practice stage, prominent timer, round controls, audio-cue concept, clear pause and stop controls.

## Candidate identity — awaiting approval
- Background near-black / obsidian #090C18
- Main action accent vivid crimson #F52F4C
- Secondary accent electric blue #4178FF
- Type ice white #F4F7FF
- Mood: cinematic premium gym atmosphere; teaching information always remains legible.
- Character: consistent appearance only as decorative concept art until qualified coach validation of actual technique media.

## Promising characteristics
Strong sense of identity; character-centered teaching; obvious next-step hierarchy; strong interaction accents; meaningful visual separation of learning vs. practice.

## Mandatory corrections before implementation
- Remove invented completed lessons, minutes and progress from a blank first-run state. Use truthful recorded state.
- Remove fake calorie-burn numbers, unvalidated estimated physiology and arbitrary +XP.
- Remove 360-degree camera actions unless true reviewed 360-degree footage exists.
- Replace 'Technique Check' (physical accuracy implication) with 'Knowledge Check' (concept comprehension only).
- Do not use an arbitrary '2 x 2 m' space minimum without source/coach justification. Use a clear and safe area prompt until a requirement is validated.
- Avoid blending stance, footwork and jab training in a session before prerequisites.
- Reduce crowded tiny text and control density for 360–430 dp Android phones; maintain readable typography and touch targets.
- Angle switchers only work when actual independent, reviewed footage is available. Generated thumbnails are NOT a validated motion rig.
- Ensure character identity continuity across every screen; current different image outputs aren't proof of one fixed instructor.
- A completed quiz or timer does NOT certify mastery or technique correctness.
- No moving decorative background that conceals hand/foot motion; honor reduced-motion and device performance limits.
- Guard against implied certification: concept media must be labelled 'Illustrative concept — not verified instruction'.

## Next design specification
1. One primary CTA per screen.
2. Explicit reviewed-media state vs. concept-only placeholder state.
3. Home statuses: not started; exploring; understood; practiced (self-reported).
4. No exposed non-working controls; disabled controls explain prerequisites.
5. Practice has working pause/stop and never falsely records an incomplete session as completed.
6. Decorative character visuals and instructional evidence separated by labeling and layout.
7. Remove unsourced training doses, artificial proficiency metrics, fake AI analysis, subscriptions and feature creep.
8. Both stance orientations need separately reviewed media.
