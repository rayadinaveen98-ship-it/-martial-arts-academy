# 04 — Premium visual direction (proposal; owner approval required)

## Experience intention
'Cinematic training arena' meets clear, frictionless educational interface. The movement, lesson purpose and next action must dominate.

## Candidate palette — NOT LOCKED
- Obsidian `#090C18`: deep background
- Crimson `#F52F4C`: critical focus and action
- Electric blue `#4178FF`: informative/interaction accent
- Ice white `#F4F7FF`: high-contrast type

Need accessibility testing; do not use dark-on-dark text or rainbow gradients without purpose.

## Visual rules
- High contrast, balanced highlights, precise spacing, expressive typography.
- Character/background may be cinematic **only while anatomical movement remains legible**.
- No filler widgets, dead cards, cluttered training catalogues or meaningless game badges.
- Use one coherent motion/illustration style for the entire prototype.
- Real instructional assets must remain distinguishable from decorative art.
- Warm-up, warnings, rest and progress states must remain readable.
- Support small phone screens, reduced-motion preference, and accessible tap targets.

## Prototyping screens for owner review
1. Training Home
2. Stance & Guard lesson studio
3. Guided session screen

### Approval workflow
Concept art -> interaction wireframe -> aligned visual screens -> owner approves -> Android UI implementation -> emulator comparison -> corrections -> final build.
