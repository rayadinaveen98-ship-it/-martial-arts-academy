# 00 — Project charter (draft)

## Mission
Teach martial arts fundamentals through technically responsible, visually striking, personalized, interactive experiences. User intent: premium polish; meaningful mechanics; rigorously accurate instructor poses; extensive research first.

## Target user (initial hypothesis)
Adult beginners who want to learn at home between / before lessons, including people returning to activity after long inactivity. This positioning needs user validation.

## Product boundaries
- v0.1 covers solo, no-contact introductory boxing literacy and low-intensity drills only.
- The app complements—does not replace—a qualified instructor, especially for partner, contact, impact and advanced technique.
- No claims that a quiz, self-report, or timer verifies physical mastery.
- No medical diagnosis, personalized rehabilitation, or unsafe fatigue challenges.

## Delivery philosophy
Research -> curriculum specification -> instructor/technique validation -> visual review -> functional prototype -> end-to-end testing -> share tested build.

## Responsible roles
- Creative/product lead: maintain coherent art direction and interaction quality.
- Engineering lead: implement only approved scope, preserve functional state.
- Technical boxing reviewer: **not yet secured**; instructional motion remains blocked pending review.
- Product owner: approves designs and scope at each visual gate.

## Research questions still open
- Which recognized coaching standard and regional instructor to adopt for technique sign-off?
- What licensed instructor footage can be sourced and under what usage terms?
- How will we handle southpaw/orthodox mirroring without false movement semantics?
- Which visual representation preserves leg position, guard, and weight transfer best?
- How much drill time suits users with no recent training? Requires validation with coach and testers.

## Success criteria (prototype)
A new user independently understands the next step, completes a knowledge check and a safe, intentionally paced practice session, sees accurate progress (not fake mastery), and can resume after app restart.
