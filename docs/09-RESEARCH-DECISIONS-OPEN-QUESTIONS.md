# 09 — Research findings, contradictions and decisions awaiting review

Updated: 2026-11-10. Not all research is complete.

## Confirmed source-grounded design findings
1. England Boxing 2026 describes stance/guard/footwork as primary coaching foundations (s. 4.4, pp. 66–68).
2. Boxing Canada 2024 organizes novice skills as stance (pp. 41–44), footwork (pp. 45–48), straight punch mechanics (pp. 49–51), jab (pp. 52–53).
3. The beginner's base and balance matter to all three; a move performed in isolation from the base should not be accepted as 'learned'.
4. Movement sequence for basic advance/retreat is different; a mirrored left/right visual treatment can be unsafe if it changes the move's semantics.
5. Both sources stress guard and return to guard after straight lead punches.
6. Beginners need progressive exposure, not an energetic random exercise catalogue (WHO guidance on inactive adults).

## Open technical conflict / nuance
**Wrist/hand orientation in the jab**
- EB26 general striking mechanics, p. 72: describes straight wrist as injury preventive.
- BC24 jab description, p. 52: describes a slightly flexed wrist for the punch.
- Coach adjudication is mandatory before any displayed wrist-angle cue, impact close-up, or teaching animation. Different context/terminology may account for wording but **we cannot assume** that without expert review.
- For the app: text can teach guard, lead-hand identification and return-to-guard now; physical-impact technique remains blocked.

## Coaching and product uncertainties
- Prefer a real instructor on screen versus 3D rig for v0.1? Research recommends real licensed and reviewed instructor, if feasible.
- Should orthdox/southpaw selection be suggested based on comfortable stance rather than handedness? Seek coach advice, let users change.
- How many minutes/repetitions are suitable for deconditioned adult beginners? Do not invent universal dose.
- What precise readiness screening questions are proportionate? Avoid medical diagnosis; enable user to opt out.
- How will users know an explainer has not been coached-reviewed? A clear 'concept preview / awaiting technical review' state is required.
- Can the art direction remain exciting without confusing cinematic action shots with instructional demonstrations?
